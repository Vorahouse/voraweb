Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026

01

What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored on a user's device when they visit certain websites. They can be used to remember preferences, support a page's technical functioning, or analyze how the website is used.

02

Cookies used on this website

The public part of this website is designed to work without analytics, advertising, or commercial tracking cookies.

Strictly necessary technical cookies may be used in private or administrative areas, for example to maintain a secure admin panel session, protect forms, or ensure the basic functioning of the service.

03

Third-party cookies

No third-party cookies are installed for advertising purposes from the public part of the website. If analytics, video, maps, social media, or other external tools are integrated in the future, this policy will be updated before such features are activated.

04

Managing cookies

Users can allow, block, or delete cookies through their browser settings. Disabling technical cookies may affect the functioning of private areas or necessary features.

05

Updates

Vora House may update this cookie policy to adapt it to technical, legal, or functional changes. The current version will always be available on this page.
